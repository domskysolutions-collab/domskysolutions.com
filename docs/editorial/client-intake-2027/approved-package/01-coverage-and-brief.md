## Editorial brief

**Working title:** AI Agent vs. Automation: What Should a Solo Business Use for Client Intake in 2027?

**Reader:** a solo service provider receiving enquiries through a form or inbox. **Decision:** keep owner handling, automate fixed steps, or trial a constrained agent that prepares work for a human. **Useful output:** one enquiry record, a reliable next-action queue and a suitable response, without silently committing to price, delivery or exceptions.

**Starting recommendation (editorial judgment):** keep manual intake while the volume and variability remain manageable. Use rules for repeated, well-defined capture, validation and templated follow-up. Trial agentic assistance only when interpreting variable enquiries demonstrably burdens the owner and the owner can review its proposals. Combining deterministic capture/routing with a narrow AI step may be sufficient; a full agent is not a mandatory next stage.

The article compares patterns, not a universal product winner. Make filters/routers and Zapier's documented AI/approval features are concrete capability examples. An agent here can choose among a small set of read/proposal tools and use their results to decide its next step; a single fixed classifier or summariser is an **AI step in a workflow**, not necessarily an agent. All external messages, final qualification, exception decisions, prices and commitments stay outside autonomous control.

### The same three cases

Use the exact fixture names and values in `fixtures.json` and protocol in `03-test-specifications.md`:

1. `INT-2027-001`: complete five-page website enquiry, EUR 2,000 budget, target 22 February 2027.
2. `INT-2027-002`: same service but budget and target date explicitly absent. Rules can detect those missing fields; an agent must not infer them from enthusiasm or defaults.
3. `INT-2027-003`: requested seven-day launch, EUR 1,000 budget, multilingual scope and a discount request. It needs owner judgment. A rule can route it for review; the agent may explain the issues, not decide that the business accepts them.

Fixture time is frozen at 4 January 2027 solely for reproducibility. Business name, people, emails, package conditions, budget numbers and requested dates are fictional test data. They are not provider prices or a forecast.
