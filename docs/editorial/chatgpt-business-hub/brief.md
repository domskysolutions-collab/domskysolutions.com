## Topic and reader decision

Title: **ChatGPT Business Hub: What Small Businesses Should Set Up First**.
Proposed route: `/blog/chatgpt-business-hub-small-business`.
Reader: a solo operator, creator or small online business deciding whether to claim a profile or launch a customer-facing agent.
Timely trigger: OpenAI's 10 November 2026 Businesses in ChatGPT beta announcement. Google's 20 October Gemini announcement supplies brief wider context, not a product ranking.
Recommendation: prepare accurate public business information, check eligibility and verification, then consider an optional agent after a bounded preview test. Buying an AI subscription, running ads or deploying an agent is not the default next action.
