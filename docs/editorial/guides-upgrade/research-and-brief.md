## Brief A — Should You Replace Adobe? A Practical Switching Checklist

**Reader decision:** Can one required creative job move while editable handoffs and recovery remain intact? Recommended opening: keep Adobe for any required task whose alternative has not passed a representative file-and-recipient check; trial one bounded job before cancelling a plan. This is editorial judgment based on compatibility and contract boundaries, not a product-quality ranking.

**Scope:** GIMP for raster/image work and Inkscape for vector work are two candidates, not replacements for the Creative Cloud suite. Do not imply either replaces InDesign, Premiere, After Effects, Acrobat, Lightroom catalog workflows, PDF accessibility, forms/signatures, plugins or a client's editable-file requirement. Keep those existing applications/services when their necessary workflows are untested. No Affinity, Canva or additional vendor catalog is needed to complete this brief.

**Proposed structure, approximately 1,300–1,700 substantive words:**

1. **Choose the job before the replacement.** Inventory deliverable, editability, collaborator, app/version, original format, linked files, fonts and printer specification. Mark each requirement mandatory/optional. Distinguish a finished PNG/PDF from a client-editable source file.
2. **Pick one candidate for one job.** Small table: web raster export→GIMP candidate; editable vector illustration→Inkscape candidate; required InDesign/Premiere/Acrobat/native-client handoff→keep existing workflow until a separate test proves coverage. Supported import is not promised round-trip fidelity.
3. **Run a file round trip.** Work on copies; open, make a known change, save a candidate-native working copy, export the agreed handoff, reopen it in the original/recipient app, and ask the recipient to perform one required edit. Log differences and keep originals. Never use a flattened export as evidence that layers/text remain editable.
4. **Check print and fonts separately.** Obtain the printer's actual PDF preset, page/bleed dimensions, color/profile, image-resolution and font requirements. Review the exported file and get the printer's acceptance; a good screen preview or passed generic preflight is insufficient. Explain Adobe Fonts' embedded/rasterized deliverable versus live-font editing distinction and packaging limits using A03, not a general font-licensing promise.
5. **Count retained costs and contract obligations.** Include remaining Adobe applications, fonts/plugins, storage, replacement subscriptions and one-time migration; keep owner minutes separate. No exact paid prices or claimed savings. Link the stack audit/calculator.
6. **Cancel only after the recovery gate.** Download/verify local copies and source assets before access/storage changes; inspect the actual account's plan, commitment, renewal, effective access end and displayed cancellation terms. Cancellation is not account deletion. Retain confirmation. Keep a documented rollback path; Adobe describes reactivation within the billing period, not indefinite reinstatement on identical terms.
7. **End with a concrete choice.** Keep, trial one task, or replace only the task that passed. A failed print/editable-source/font check means keep the relevant workflow while resolving it.
