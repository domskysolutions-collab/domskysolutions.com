# Domsky visual identity

Use the semantic ivory/forest system in `src/index.css`: ivory page, charcoal ink, forest actions/links and sparse terracotta emphasis. The November color redesign supersedes the former orange palette. Reuse existing fonts; no new dependencies.

Lead with a decision rather than a trust statistic. Prefer an asymmetric lead and short reading list over interchangeable feature cards. Use dividers to separate stories; forest green identifies links, focus and restrained labels. Keep useful cards in working tools. Avoid blanket removal of grids, tables or callouts.

Body copy should remain at least 16px with a comfortable reading measure and 1.7–1.85 line height. Keep visible focus, semantic headings, contrast, 200% text enlargement and mobile wrapping. Wide tables use labeled, focusable overflow regions and row/column headers; the page itself must not scroll horizontally.

## Controlled variation

- Comparisons: decision and evidence scope first, equal criteria, workflow/recovery details.
- Reviews: capabilities, sources and observations are distinct; no obligatory score or pros/cons card.
- Tutorials: prerequisites, steps, expected output and recovery; mark demonstrations clearly.
- Guides: named situation and decision path; no forced purchase.
- Opinion: explicit argument, evidence and limits; not a disguised feature inventory.

Pilot typography and evidence-first ordering on one comparison with scoped CSS. Broader adoption needs review. Do not mandate verdict/table/FAQ combinations.

Retain valid images and honest alt text. Label diagrams as editorial diagrams. Screenshots need capture date, plan and provenance; never generate fake product UI as evidence. A nonessential cover can follow the opening decision without changing its URL or metadata. Do not add glow, gradients, animation or badges just to create visual interest.

The finder and newsletter remain working components. Preserve logic, consent, signup, analytics, storage, tracking and anchors. Intro-heading semantics may adapt to the containing page without changing recommendations.
